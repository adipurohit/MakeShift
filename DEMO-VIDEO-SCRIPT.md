# MakeShift — 45-Second Demo Video Script

A produced video, not a live stage pitch (that's `PITCH.md`'s 3:30 version — use this one for a submitted/social video). Cuts between **SCREEN** (app recording) and **LIVE** (real hands assembling the real Robot Arm). Default example is the Robot Arm build since it's the guaranteed, fully-offline path and the most visually satisfying to film — swap in your actual build if you film something else.

Total runtime: **45 seconds**. Voiceover is deliberately sparse during the live-build montage — let the real footage do the work there; don't talk over it.

A rendered draft (`docs/demo-video-draft.mp4`) exists in the repo with real screen-recorded app footage and real voiceover, and clearly-labeled placeholder cards exactly where the LIVE beats go — see "About the rendered draft" below before you film. (There's also a separate, older `docs/demo.mp4` / `docs/demo.gif` in the repo — a silent screen-recording with no voiceover and no live-footage beats, used for the README. This script's draft is a different thing: built specifically from the 45-second voiceover script below.)

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | VO (read exactly as written, or close to it) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | **LIVE** — close-up on the actual scrap: a dead 3D printer, stepper motors, a bent nozzle | "Two dead 3D printers. Everyone else saw e-waste." | Hands-in-frame, real parts, no app yet. Hook on the problem, not the product. |
| 0:04–0:10 | **SCREEN** — open MakeShift, click Scan Material → Upload a photo instead → pick a photo of the parts | "MakeShift saw a robot arm. Upload a photo—" | Upload is far more reliable to screen-record than a live camera permission prompt + video feed. Speed the recording up slightly if it drags. |
| 0:10–0:17 | **SCREEN** — Recipe Catalog → select Robot Arm → 2D merge canvas, a part or two snapping together, hologram chips turning from dashed to solid green | "—and it shows you exactly what to build, piece by piece." | The one beat that has to sell the actual product mechanic — don't rush the merge-to-green moment. |
| 0:17–0:32 | **LIVE** — fast-cut montage of real hands actually assembling the robot arm: bolting a joint, threading a belt, wiring the board | *(mostly silent — ambient build sounds: a drill, a belt snapping taut, a click)* "Then you build it for real." | 15 seconds, jump-cut through the real steps. This is the emotional core — give it room. One short VO line only, dropped in partway through. |
| 0:32–0:40 | **SCREEN** — Build It → steps checked off → Mark Build Complete → confetti/badge. Hard cut to **LIVE**: the finished physical arm, moving | "From scrap to something that actually works." | The screen-to-live cut here is the payoff shot — app says "done," then you immediately show it's real. |
| 0:40–0:45 | **LIVE or SCREEN** — finished arm in frame, logo/wordmark card | "MakeShift." | Hold the final frame past the VO — don't cut on the last word. |

## Full voiceover, read continuously (for recording in one take)

> Two dead 3D printers. Everyone else saw e-waste.
> MakeShift saw a robot arm. Upload a photo — and it shows you exactly what to build, piece by piece.
> Then you build it for real.
> From scrap to something that actually works.
> MakeShift.

~45 words total. Read it out loud once with a stopwatch before filming — if it's running long, the hook and the live-build line are the two safest to trim; don't cut the tagline.

## About the rendered draft (`docs/demo-video-draft.mp4`)

What it actually is: the three SCREEN beats above, captured from the real running app (upload-photo flow, Robot Arm recipe selection, the 2D merge/hologram view, and the Build Complete/confetti screen), with real voiceover generated from the script above. The three LIVE beats are dark title cards reading what footage goes there and for how long — **not** stand-ins meant to ship, just timing scaffolding.

To finish it: open `docs/demo-video-draft.mp4` in any video editor (iMovie, CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci — anything that can do a simple cut), find each "LIVE FOOTAGE" card, and replace it with your actual filmed clip trimmed to roughly that card's duration. The screen segments and voiceover are already real and don't need re-recording unless you want to.

## Filming notes

- **Burn in captions.** Social/submission videos get watched muted on a first pass far more often than with sound — don't make the pitch depend on audio.
- **Shoot more live-build footage than you need.** You only need ~15 seconds in the final cut, but real assembly rarely looks great on the first take — overshoot and pick the best bolts-turning, belt-threading, wire-connecting shots in edit.
- **Match the cut, not just the VO, to the beat changes above** — a hard cut from SCREEN to LIVE lands harder than a crossfade for this kind of proof-it's-real moment (especially the 0:32 beat).
- **Light the live-build shots well.** A phone camera with good light beats a nicer camera with bad light — use a window or a desk lamp, not overhead fluorescents if you can help it.
- Do one full dry run of the whole 45 seconds — screen + live — before the real shoot, same as the live-pitch rehearsal in `PITCH.md`.
