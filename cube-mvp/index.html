    How it works

    Turn scrap into a working build

    MakeShift treats broken gear as a parts inventory. Every recipe is a real build you merge together piece by piece.

    1. 1
      Pick a recipe

      Browse 101 builds in the Recipe Catalog. Each one loads its own parts.

    2. 2
      Merge parts

      Drag two parts onto the bench. Parts that fit together merge into a sub-assembly, like a Motor and Pulley into a Drive Unit.

    3. 3
      Fill the checklist

      The bar under the bench tracks what the build needs. Each item turns green once you have enough.

    4. 4
      Build it

      Press Build It, check off the assembly steps, add a photo, and earn a badge.
